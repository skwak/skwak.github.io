## Academic research tools
- **Web of Science** ([https://www.webofscience.com](https://www.webofscience.com/wos/woscc/smart-search))  
  purpose: Search scholarly publications and explore citation information

- **Master Journal List** ([https://mjl.clarivate.com](https://mjl.clarivate.com/home))  
  purpose: Check whether academic journals are indexded in Web of Science

- **Journal Citation Reports** ([https://jcr.clarivate.com](https://jcr.clarivate.com/jcr/home))  
  purpose: Find the impact factor, quartile, and ranking of academic journals

## Latex

- **Detexify** ([https://detexify.kirelabs.org](https://detexify.kirelabs.org))  
  purpose: Find the LaTeX command for a symbol you can draw but not name
